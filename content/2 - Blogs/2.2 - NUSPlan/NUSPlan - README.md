## Motivation
Personally, I spent most of my Y1S1 finding the right productivity application for my academic use, but none of them was tailored to my needs. While Google Calendar was what I needed for schedule management, the features of the integrated Google Task felt like it was missing customisability. Likewise, Microsoft Outlook felt inferior compared to Google Calendar, but its task management with Microsoft To Do had many more useful features. I also ended up exploring Notion, but setting it up was too cumbersome and time-consuming.

This led to my partner and I wanting to develop a new productivity platform, which encompasses all of the best features from each existing productivity application, while adding more features which would be beneficial for NUS students, especially future Year 1 students who may face the same struggle as us.

# Installation guide

## Deployment

Refer to the attached APK file: [apk.file](https://drive.google.com/file/d/15mjzKkI2EIQ9v2VgQYWoG-DtWvlyLiAw/view?usp=drive_link)
## Set up Instructions
1. Download the APK from our google drive using the link above into your android device
2. Ensure that your device allows installation of unknown apps
3. Open the APK file and follow the install instructions
4. Once installed, open to launch the app

If you face any issues with installing the APK, you can email us at [nusplanorbital@gmail.com](mailto:nusplanorbital@gmail.com)
